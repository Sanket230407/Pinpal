How PinPal works

  1. Share your location

    Press one button when you want to appear. PinPal never tracks you in the background.

  2. Find people like you

    Search up to 25 km away and filter by interests such as Music, Coding or Sports.

  3. Connect and chat

    Send a request. Once it's accepted, you can message each other in real time.

You decide what people see

Other people can see

  • Your name, username, bio and interests
  • A rounded distance, such as 1.2 km
  • A map pin placed 200–400 m from your real location

Other people can't see

  • Your exact location
  • Your email address
  • Your profile, once you block them

Want a break? Turn on Ghost Mode and you disappear from Nearby straight away.
